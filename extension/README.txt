Qalaisyn for Chrome — how to install (1 minute)
1. Unzip this folder anywhere.
2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome and turn on "Developer mode" (top right).
3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the unzipped folder "qalaisyn-extension".
4. Open any site (Google Translate, Gmail, WhatsApp Web...), click into a text field,
   type Kazakh the way you normally do on a Russian keyboard, and press Alt+Q
   (or click the Q icon in the toolbar). The letters are put back right in the field.
Everything runs on your computer; no text is sent anywhere.
